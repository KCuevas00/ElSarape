Our Story

El Sarape is a family-owned restaurant that has been serving authentic Mexican cuisine for over 20 years. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, preserving the traditional cooking methods and flavors that make Mexican food so special. We treat every customer like family and take pride in creating a warm, welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels at home.
