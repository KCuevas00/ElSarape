Our Story

Learn about our history and traditions

El Sarape was founded with a simple goal: to bring authentic Mexican flavors to our community. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, preserving the traditional cooking methods and ingredients that make Mexican cuisine so special.

We source fresh ingredients whenever possible and prepare each dish with care. From our handmade tortillas to our slow-cooked meats, everything is made in-house to ensure the highest quality.

Our restaurant is named after the traditional Mexican garment, the sarape, which represents the vibrant colors and rich culture of Mexico. We hope our food brings a piece of that tradition to your table.
